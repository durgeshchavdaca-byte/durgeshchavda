What no calculator can decide for you

  • Whether the money was “taxed or disclosed”. This decides between ₹1 lakh and 60%. An asset paid for even partly from untaxed income cannot go in Category B (question 70).
  • Your residential status in each year. The year you left India and the year you came back are where most mistakes happen.
  • Which assets you must list. Joint accounts, accounts you only sign for, and assets where you are a beneficiary can all count (questions 56 to 61).
  • What you cannot fix later. Only one Form 1 counts. It can be refiled until Form 2 is issued. After that nothing can be added, and immunity covers only what was declared (questions 53, 54 and 84 to 86).

Frequently asked questions

How is the FAST-DS amount calculated?
Category A (a foreign asset bought from untaxed income, or foreign income not offered to tax, up to ₹1 crore in total) pays tax at 30% plus an additional amount at 30%, 60% in all. Category B (an asset from taxed income, or acquired while non-resident or RNOR, only missing from Schedule FA, up to ₹5 crore in total) pays one flat fee of ₹1 lakh. Both can be in one Form 1.
What is the difference between Category A and Category B in FAST-DS?
Category A is for undisclosed foreign assets or foreign income that was never offered to tax. Category B is for assets bought from taxed income, or acquired while non-resident or RNOR, that were only left out of Schedule FA or where no return was filed. An asset funded even partly from untaxed income cannot be declared in Category B; it can be declared in Category A, if eligible.
How is a foreign bank account valued under FAST-DS?
At the sum of all deposits from the date the account was opened to 31 March 2026, not the closing balance. Deposits made out of money withdrawn earlier from the same account are excluded, and so are deposits made before an earlier Black Money Act declaration of the account on which tax and penalty were charged.
Which exchange rate applies for FAST-DS?
The RBI reference rate on the valuation date. As no rate was published on 31 March 2026, the Department has said the rate of ₹94.6543 per US dollar published on 30 March 2026 is to be used.
Do I pay ₹1 lakh for each asset or for each year under Category B?
No. It is a single flat fee of ₹1 lakh for all Category B assets and all years, as long as their total value in your Form 1 does not exceed ₹5 crore.
Do small foreign bank accounts need a FAST-DS declaration?
Not where the combined balance of your foreign bank accounts did not exceed ₹20 lakh at any time in the year and the deposits came from disclosed sources. A declaration is needed if the account holds income that was not taxed.
I missed reporting a foreign asset only in FY 2025-26. Do I need FAST-DS?
The Department's Supplementary FAQs say a revised return for assessment year 2026-27, showing the income and the asset in Schedule FA and filed on or before 31 March 2027, is treated as sufficient compliance. This applies only where no earlier resident year is involved.
What happens if I don't pay the FAST-DS amount on time?
You must pay within two months from the end of the month in which you receive Form 2. A further two months is allowed with simple interest at 1% for every month or part of a month. After that the benefit of the scheme is lost for that declaration.
