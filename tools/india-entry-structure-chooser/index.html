Frequently asked questions

Which is better for a foreign company in India: a branch or a subsidiary?
Usually a subsidiary: it is taxed at a lower rate (22% base option versus 35% for a branch), limits the parent's liability and can do any business allowed under FDI rules. A branch suits companies that must operate as the same legal entity.
Can a liaison office in India earn income?
No. A liaison office can only act as a communication channel — representing the parent, researching the market and promoting collaboration. Its expenses are funded from abroad.
Is an employer of record a substitute for an Indian entity?
For a few hires while you test the market, yes. It does not let you invoice Indian customers, and if staff conclude contracts for you it can create permanent-establishment risk.
