Gold tax calculator: tax on selling gold in 2026

Selling old jewellery before Dhanteras (6 November 2026), or booking profit on gold ETFs? See the tax on your gain, then read the rules for buying, gifting and holding gold.

How gold is taxed when you sell

Type of goldLong-term afterLong-term taxShort-term tax
Jewellery, coins, bars, digital gold24 months12.5%, no indexationYour slab rate
Gold ETFs (listed)12 months12.5%Your slab rate
Gold mutual funds (fund of funds)24 months12.5%Your slab rate

Plus 4% cess, and surcharge if your income is above ₹50 lakh (capped at 15% on long-term gains). Keep the original bill: without it, proving your cost is hard.

Buying gold this Dhanteras

  • GST: 3% on the value of the gold, plus 5% on making charges.
  • PAN: needed for jewellery purchases of ₹2 lakh or more.
  • Cash: a jeweller cannot accept ₹2 lakh or more in cash from you for one purchase; pay by card, UPI or bank transfer.
  • Exchanging old jewellery: swapping old gold for new is treated as selling the old piece, so a gain can arise.

Gold as a gift

Gold received from relatives, or on your marriage, is not taxed. Gifts from friends and others are taxed as income if their total value in the year is above ₹50,000. When you later sell gifted or inherited gold, the original owner’s cost and holding period carry over to you.

How much gold can you keep at home?

There is no legal limit on owning gold you can explain. Under CBDT Instruction No. 1916 (1994), during a tax search officers do not seize jewellery up to 500 g for a married woman, 250 g for an unmarried woman and 100 g for a man, even without documents. Above that, keep bills, gift records or inheritance papers.

NRIs bringing gold to India: customs rules apply. See NRI tax services. Selling property instead? Use the property capital gains calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the tax on selling gold jewellery in 2026?
If you held it for more than 24 months, the gain is long-term and taxed at 12.5% without indexation, plus cess. If you held it for 24 months or less, the gain is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate.
Is there tax on gold ETF profit?
Yes. Gold ETFs held for more than 12 months are taxed at 12.5% on the gain; within 12 months the gain is taxed at your slab rate.
How much gold can I keep at home in India?
There is no legal limit if you can explain the source. Under CBDT Instruction 1916, officers do not seize up to 500 g for a married woman, 250 g for an unmarried woman and 100 g for a man during a search, even without documents.
Is gold received as a gift taxable?
Not if it comes from a relative or on your marriage. Gifts from others are taxable if their total value in the year exceeds ₹50,000.
Is PAN required to buy gold on Dhanteras?
Yes, for jewellery purchases of ₹2 lakh or more. Jewellers also cannot accept ₹2 lakh or more in cash for a purchase.
