Frequently asked questions

Is income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free in 2026-27?
For resident individuals under the new regime, yes — the section 156 (old 87A) rebate of up to ₹60,000 covers tax on taxable income up to ₹12 lakh; with the ₹75,000 standard deduction, salary up to ₹12.75 lakh is effectively tax-free. It doesn't apply to NRIs or to special-rate income like capital gains.
Which is better, the new or old tax regime?
The new regime is better for most people unless old-regime deductions — 80C, 80D, home-loan interest, HRA — are large. The calculator compares both for your numbers.
What are the income tax slabs for 2026-27?
New regime: nil up to ₹4 lakh, then 5% to ₹8 lakh, 10% to ₹12 lakh, 15% to ₹16 lakh, 20% to ₹20 lakh, 25% to ₹24 lakh and 30% above. Budget 2026 made no change.
