Frequently asked questions

Is AEO allowed for CA firms under ICAI rules?
ICAI restricts advertising and solicitation by members in practice, but permits factual information and educational content on a firm's website within its guidelines. Any AEO work should stay within those rules; check the current ICAI guidelines for your situation.
Which questions should an accounting firm target for AEO?
The specific questions prospective clients ask: services by city, specific forms and deadlines, cross-border tax situations and 'who can help me with…' questions in your niche.
