Indian CA in Dubai — for NRIs and UAE businesses with India ties

From our office in Business Bay, CA Durgesh Chavda helps UAE-based NRIs and businesses with everything on the India side: tax returns, property sales, repatriation, investing in India, Indian companies and GIFT City.

Talk to our Dubai office hello@durgeshchavda.com

For NRIs in the UAE

  • Indian income-tax returns for rent, NRO interest, capital gains and dividends — and refunds of excess TDS. See NRI taxation.
  • Selling property in India: lower-TDS certificates (Form 128) and the capital-gains computation — TDS on property sale by an NRI.
  • Moving money: NRE / NRO / FCNR planning and repatriation up to USD 1 million a year — NRO repatriation.
  • Residential status for frequent travellers and the ₹15 lakh deemed-residence rule for people in no-tax countries — check yours.
  • Returning to India: RNOR planning and foreign-asset reporting, including FAST-DS 2026.

For UAE businesses and founders

  • Indian subsidiaries and branches for UAE companies — company setup in India.
  • GIFT City funds and family-office structures for UAE-based families.
  • Due diligence and supplier checks on Indian counterparties — due diligence in India.
  • Indian startups raising from Gulf investors — FEMA filings and valuation.

India–UAE tax treaty in brief

  • Dividends from India to a UAE resident: 10% under the treaty (with a tax residency certificate and Form 41), instead of 20% plus surcharge and cess.
  • Because the UAE has no personal income tax, residency evidence matters — keep your UAE tax residency certificate current.
  • UAE corporate tax, VAT and audits are handled by UAE-licensed professionals; we coordinate with them on the India side.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an Indian CA in Dubai for NRI tax?
Yes. CA Durgesh Chavda, a Chartered Accountant (ICAI), has an office in Business Bay, Dubai, and handles Indian tax returns, property sales, repatriation and investments for UAE-based NRIs.
Do NRIs in Dubai pay tax in India?
Only on Indian income — rent, NRO interest, capital gains and dividends. An Indian citizen with Indian income over ₹15 lakh who isn't taxed anywhere else can be treated as resident (RNOR), which matters for UAE residents.
What is the dividend tax rate for UAE residents under the India–UAE DTAA?
10% under the treaty, with a tax residency certificate and Form 41, compared with 20% plus surcharge and cess without it.
