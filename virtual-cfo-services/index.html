What a Virtual CFO does for you

Most early-stage companies have bookkeeping and a tax consultant. What they don’t have is someone who turns those numbers into decisions — how much runway you really have, which channel is actually profitable, and what investors will ask in the next board meeting. That is the job of a Virtual CFO.

  • 3-statement financial models — P&L, balance sheet and cash flow, linked and scenario-ready.
  • Monthly MIS and board packs — a consistent monthly close with an investor update deck your board can read in ten minutes.
  • Runway and burn monitoring — cash forecasts that flag problems months before they become emergencies.
  • Unit economics dashboards — CAC, LTV, contribution margin and payback, by channel and cohort.
  • Budget vs actuals — variance tracking and quarterly reforecasting.
  • Compliance oversight — GST, TDS, advance tax, ROC and FEMA calendars owned, not chased.

Who it’s for

Virtual CFO support works best for companies that have outgrown “the CA does our returns” but don’t yet need (or can’t yet justify) a full-time CFO:

  • VC-backed startups from seed to Series A that owe investors regular, reliable reporting.
  • Founders preparing to raise and needing an investor-grade model and data room.
  • Companies with foreign shareholders or a US / Singapore / UAE parent, where FEMA and cross-border reporting add complexity.
  • Family offices and growing businesses that want institutional-grade reporting.

Frequently asked questions

What does a Virtual CFO do?
A Virtual CFO provides senior finance leadership on a part-time or retainer basis: financial models, monthly MIS and board packs, cash-flow and runway forecasting, unit economics, budgeting, fundraising support and oversight of tax and regulatory compliance.
How is a Virtual CFO different from an accountant?
An accountant records transactions and files returns. A Virtual CFO uses that data to guide decisions — forecasting, pricing, fundraising and investor reporting — and owns the finance function end-to-end.
Do you work with startups outside Ahmedabad?
Yes. Engagements run remotely for clients across India and abroad, with offices in Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Rajkot and Dubai.
Can a Virtual CFO help us raise funding?
Yes — fundraising support is a core part of the role: investor-grade model, cap table, data room, due-diligence management and term-sheet review. Durgesh led a USD 3.75M Pre-Series A end-to-end.
