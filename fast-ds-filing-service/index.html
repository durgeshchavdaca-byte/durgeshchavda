FAST-DS filing, done for you before 31 December

Foreign bank account, RSUs from a US employer, savings from your years abroad, never shown in your Indian return? FAST-DS is a one-time window to put it right at a known cost. We handle the whole filing, from valuation to the final certificate.

Who we help

  • Employees of foreign companies with RSUs, ESPP or ESOP shares at E*Trade, Morgan Stanley, Fidelity, Schwab or similar, never reported in Schedule FA.
  • Returning NRIs with bank accounts, pensions or investments from their years abroad.
  • Former students with an account left open in the US, UK, Canada or Australia.
  • Families with foreign property, insurance or deposits that were never reported.
  • Current NRIs who were resident in India when the asset was acquired or the income arose.

What we do

  1. Review: list every foreign account, security, RSU, policy and property, and check which ones actually need FAST-DS. Some do not: for example where only the ₹20 lakh Schedule FA threshold matters.
  2. Classify: decide between Category 1 (60%) and Category 2 (₹1 lakh) for each item, and check the ₹1 crore and ₹5 crore limits.
  3. Value: compute the value as on 31 March 2026 under the scheme rules, including the sum-of-deposits rule for bank accounts and RBI reference rates.
  4. File Form 1 on the e-filing portal, with the supporting evidence.
  5. Check Form 2 (the order of the amount payable), make the payment through challan ITNS 289 with you, and file Form 3 within the deadline.
  6. Follow through to Form 4 (the payment certificate), and set up correct Schedule FA reporting in your future returns.

Documents we need

  • PAN and e-filing login, and your Indian returns for the years concerned.
  • Bank statements for each foreign account from opening to 31 March 2026.
  • Brokerage statements and vesting or purchase records for shares, RSUs and ESPP.
  • Purchase documents for property and policy documents for insurance or pensions.
  • Your travel dates for the relevant years, to confirm residential status.

Frequently asked questions

Can you file FAST-DS for me?
Yes. We review your foreign assets, value them as on 31 March 2026, choose the right category, file Form 1, help you pay the amount in Form 2 through challan ITNS 289, file Form 3 and follow through to the Form 4 certificate.
What is the last date for FAST-DS?
31 December 2026. No declaration can be filed after that date, so leave time to collect statements and complete the valuation.
How much does FAST-DS cost?
The scheme amount is 60% of the value for undisclosed assets or income up to ₹1 crore (Category 1), or a flat ₹1 lakh where the asset came from taxed income or was acquired while non-resident and was only missing from Schedule FA, up to ₹5 crore (Category 2). Our professional fee is fixed after a short review.
Do I need FAST-DS if my foreign assets are small?
Not always. Since 1 October 2024 the ₹10 lakh Schedule FA penalty does not apply where foreign assets other than immovable property total ₹20 lakh or less, although you must still report them. We check whether FAST-DS is worth it for you before filing.
Can an NRI file FAST-DS?
Yes, if you were resident in India in the year the foreign asset was acquired or the foreign income arose, even if you are a non-resident today.
