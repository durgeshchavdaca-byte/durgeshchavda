What we don’t do

We’re Chartered Accountants, so legal opinions, litigation in courts and immigration work stay with lawyers — we coordinate with them. We don’t act as a nominee shareholder or sign as a director on your behalf, and we don’t do anything that would compromise our independence as auditors or advisers.

Frequently asked questions

Who can do work in India on behalf of a foreign company?
A Chartered Accountant firm with cross-border experience can handle most finance, tax, regulatory filing, research and verification work. CA Durgesh Chavda's team acts for foreign companies remotely and on the ground from Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Rajkot and Dubai, reporting in English.
Can you represent a foreign company before Indian tax authorities?
Yes. Chartered Accountants can act as authorised representatives in income-tax and GST proceedings — replying to notices, attending hearings and handling appeals — for foreign companies and NRIs.
Can you visit a supplier or company in India for us?
Yes. We combine public-record checks with a visit to the premises and a written report with photographs — see supplier verification.
Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes. We work with clients in the US, UK, Europe, the Gulf, Singapore, Japan, Canada and Australia, remotely and with calls on their time zone.
