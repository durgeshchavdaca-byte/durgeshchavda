Frequently asked questions

How much does an India Company Check cost?
USD 249 per company, with a written English report within 5 working days of confirming the company's details. An optional site visit is quoted separately.
What is an India Company Check?
A fixed-scope verification of one Indian company from public records — registrations, filed accounts, charges, directors and payment details — with an optional site visit and a short English report.
How is it different from due diligence?
It answers whether a company is real, solvent-looking and roughly what it claims. Full due diligence adds a deep financial, tax and FEMA review, management meetings and deal recommendations.
