Family office guide

Setting up a family office in India: onshore, GIFT City or both

India has no separate licence called “family office”. Families choose a structure based on where the money is, where it’s going and who needs to invest alongside them.

By CA Durgesh Chavda·Last reviewed 26 September 2026·7 min read

The four main routes

The GIFT City Family Investment Fund

  • A self-managed fund for a single family, registered with IFSCA.
  • Its manager needs to be at least an Authorised FME (minimum net worth USD 75,000).
  • The fund must reach a corpus of USD 10 million within three years of registration.
  • It operates in foreign currency from the IFSC and can invest in India and abroad, with the IFSC tax incentives — see GIFT City tax benefits.

Resident Indian families going global

  • Individuals can remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year each under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme; since July 2024 RBI allows LRS remittances to IFSCs for all permissible purposes.
  • TCS of 20% applies to investment remittances above ₹10 lakh a year — creditable against income tax, but it ties up cash.
  • Companies and LLPs investing abroad follow the overseas investment rules instead — see ODI rules.
  • Confirm with the authorised dealer bank how a specific FIF investment is treated before remitting.

NRI families investing in India

  • NRIs invest as non-residents — directly in Indian companies (with FEMA reporting), through AIFs, or through GIFT City funds.
  • For families in the UAE, see our UAE page.

Running the office

  • Consolidated reporting across entities and countries, investment committee packs and cash forecasting.
  • Tax: capital gains planning, treaty claims, Schedule FA disclosures for residents.
  • Governance: a family charter, investment policy and succession planning with lawyers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set up a family office in India?
There's no separate family office licence. Families typically use a holding company or LLP for Indian investments, a SEBI AIF to pool capital, or a GIFT City Family Investment Fund for foreign-currency investments; many use a combination.
What is a Family Investment Fund in GIFT City?
A self-managed fund for a single family registered with IFSCA in GIFT City. It must reach a corpus of USD 10 million within three years and is managed by at least an Authorised FME with USD 75,000 net worth.
Can a resident Indian family invest through GIFT City?
Resident individuals can remit up to USD 250,000 each per financial year under LRS to IFSCs for permissible purposes, with 20% TCS on investment remittances above ₹10 lakh. Check the treatment of the specific investment with the authorised dealer bank.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
