Delaware vs Wyoming LLC: which state for an India-based founder?

Wyoming is cheaper to keep alive. Delaware is what investors recognise. For most India-based founders the deciding question isn’t the state at all — it’s whether you should be an LLC.

By CA Durgesh Chavda·Last reviewed 26 September 2026·6 min read

On state charges alone, Wyoming is usually several hundred dollars a year cheaper. Registered-agent fees are similar in both.

Side-by-side on what actually matters

WyomingDelaware
State income tax on the LLCNoneGenerally none for an LLC with no Delaware operations
Investor familiarityLowHigh — the default for funds and acquirers

Best forLow-cost, owner-run businessesAnything that may take investors or be sold

When Wyoming fits

  • A one-person services or consulting business invoicing US clients.
  • A US storefront for an Indian product with no plans to raise capital.
  • A holding vehicle where running cost matters more than investor optics.

When Delaware fits

  • You may raise money, add co-owners or sell the business.
  • Counterparties (enterprise customers, platforms, lenders) expect Delaware.
  • You want predictable, well-tested law for disputes between owners.

What’s the same in both states

  • Form 5472: a foreign-owned single-member LLC files a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 every year, whichever state it is in. Read the Form 5472 guide.
  • Where you operate: if you have an office or employees in another state (say California or Texas), you register and may pay tax there — the formation state doesn’t change that.
  • India side: your investment is an overseas investment under FEMA, reported through your AD bank, and the holding goes into Schedule FA of your income-tax return. See the ODI guide.
  • No BOI report: US-formed LLCs no longer file beneficial-ownership reports with FinCEN.

First: should you be an LLC at all?

For India-resident owners, an LLC that the US treats as “disregarded” may still be treated as a separate foreign company for Indian tax — which can make foreign tax credits harder to claim. And if there’s any chance you’ll raise venture capital, you’ll need a C-Corp anyway. Compare before you choose a state: Delaware C-Corp vs LLC, or go straight to US company registration from India.

Frequently asked questions

Is a Wyoming LLC cheaper than a Delaware LLC?
Usually yes. Wyoming charges a $100 filing fee and a $60 minimum annual report licence tax, while Delaware charges a flat $400 annual LLC tax from 2026, plus its formation fee. Registered-agent costs are similar.
What is the Delaware LLC annual tax in 2026?
$400, due by 1 June. Delaware raised it from $300 under House Bill 400, effective for the 2026 tax year. Late payment attracts a $200 penalty plus 1.5% interest per month.
When is the Wyoming LLC annual report due?
On the first day of the LLC's anniversary month — the month it was formed.
Does a Wyoming LLC owned by an Indian need to file Form 5472?
Yes. A foreign-owned single-member LLC files a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 annually, regardless of the state of formation.
Which state should I choose if I plan to raise funding?
Delaware — and usually as a C-Corp rather than an LLC, since most venture investors require a Delaware C-Corp.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
