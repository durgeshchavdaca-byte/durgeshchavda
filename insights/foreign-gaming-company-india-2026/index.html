Foreign gaming companies in India: what you can and cannot set up after the 2025 law

International gaming groups still approach Indian advisers for company set-up, bank accounts and directors. Since August 2025 the answer depends entirely on what kind of game you offer. Here is the line India’s Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 draws, what foreign groups can build in India, and what any reputable adviser will refuse to do.

Key takeaways
  • Online money games are banned in India since August 2025.
  • E-sports, social games and game studios remain open to foreign groups.
  • Resident directors must have a real role; avoid nominee arrangements.
Quick answer: the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 and its Rules, in force since 1 May 2026, ban online money games in India, whether based on skill or chance, including offshore platforms accessible from India. Offering, advertising or processing payments for them can lead to imprisonment of up to three years and fines of up to INR 1 crore, and banks must block such transactions. Separately, foreign investment in gambling and betting is prohibited. Foreign groups can still set up in India for e-sports, online social and casual games without stakes, game development studios and technology services.

Where the line is

ActivityPosition in India
Real-money games (fantasy with cash entry, poker, rummy, casino, betting) offered to users in IndiaProhibited under the 2025 Act; no FDI in gambling or betting
Advertising or promoting real-money games, including by influencersProhibited
Banks or payment intermediaries processing payments for themProhibited; banks actively monitor and block
E-sports (competitive games, tournaments)Permitted and promoted under the Act
Online social, casual and educational games with no money stakesPermitted
Game development studio, art, QA, live-ops for non-money gamesPermitted; 100% FDI possible under the automatic route for software and IT services

If a group builds or operates any money-gaming product for other countries from India, take a specific legal opinion first: the Act also reaches platforms accessible in India and anyone facilitating them.

The Rules also set up the Online Gaming Authority of India under MeitY to classify and register games and to enforce the law, including against offshore platforms.

Setting up a permitted gaming business

  1. Entity: an Indian private limited subsidiary (or LLP) with a clear business object limited to permitted gaming, development or technology services.
  2. FDI reporting: FC-GPR within 30 days of share allotment; land-border-country investors need government approval.
  3. Directors: at least one director resident in India (182 days or more in the year) with a real role. Directors carry personal liability, so a “nominee” who only lends a name is a serious risk for everyone involved.
  4. Banking: expect enhanced due diligence on any gaming-related account; a clean, documented business model is essential.
  5. Tax: transfer pricing for services to the parent, GST on Indian revenue and withholding on cross-border payments.

What a reputable adviser will not do

  • Incorporate or open bank accounts for an online money-gaming or betting business aimed at India.
  • Provide “nominee” or dummy directors who take no part in the business.
  • Route payments to disguise their purpose.

Frequently asked questions

Is online gaming legal in India for foreign companies?
Online money games, whether skill or chance, are banned under the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025, and foreign investment in gambling and betting is prohibited. E-sports, online social and casual games without money stakes, and game development or technology services are permitted.
What is the penalty for offering online money games in India?
Offering or facilitating online money games can lead to imprisonment of up to three years and a fine of up to INR 1 crore under the 2025 Act, and banks and payment intermediaries must not process such transactions.
Can a foreign gaming company get a nominee director in India?
Every Indian company needs at least one director resident in India, but directors carry personal legal liability. A nominee who only lends a name, especially for a business that may breach the online gaming law, is a serious risk, and reputable advisers do not provide one.
Can a foreign company open a game development studio in India?
Yes. A studio building non-money games, art, QA or live-ops can be set up as an Indian subsidiary with up to 100% foreign investment under the automatic route, with FDI reporting, a resident director and transfer pricing for services to the parent.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
