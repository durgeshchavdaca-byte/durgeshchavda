Indian living in Russia? What India still taxes, and at what rate

Thousands of Indian students, engineers and professionals live in Russia. Once you are a non-resident in India, India only taxes your Indian income: rent, NRO interest, dividends and gains on Indian assets. The India-Russia tax treaty can cut the Indian tax on interest and dividends to 10%, but only if you claim it. Here is what applies, and the practical points about money transfers since 2022.

Key takeaways
  • NRIs in Russia pay Indian tax only on Indian income.
  • Treaty rate of 10% on interest and dividends needs a Russian TRC and Form 41; otherwise NRO interest suffers 30%.
  • Russia taxes its residents on worldwide income: claim credit there for Indian tax.
Quick answer: as an NRI living in Russia you pay Indian tax only on Indian income. Under the India-Russia tax treaty (in force since 1998), Indian tax on dividends, interest and royalties is capped at 10% if you give your Indian bank or company a Russian tax residency certificate and Form 41. Without them, NRO interest suffers 30% plus surcharge and cess. India keeps the right to tax your gains on Indian shares and property. Russia taxes its residents on worldwide income, so you claim credit there for the Indian tax.

How Russia taxes you

  • You are a Russian tax resident if you spend 183 days or more in Russia in 12 consecutive months.
  • Russian residents are taxed on worldwide income. From 2025 the rates are progressive, from 13% up to 22% for the highest incomes; non-residents generally pay 30% on Russian income.
  • Indian income you earn (interest, rent, dividends, gains) is reportable in Russia; the treaty lets you credit the Indian tax against Russian tax on the same income.

Moving money between India and Russia

  • Since March 2022 several Russian banks have been cut off from SWIFT, and transfers involving Russia face closer checks. Expect delays and more questions from your bank.
  • From India, money in your NRO account can go abroad up to USD 1 million a year with the CA certificate and Form 145: see NRI money transfer. Talk to your Indian bank before a large transfer so the paperwork is ready.
  • Keep salary and savings from Russia in an NRE account in India: interest is tax-free in India and the money stays freely repatriable.

Checklist for Indians in Russia

  1. Update your residential status with your Indian bank and switch savings to NRE.
  2. Get a Russian tax residency certificate and file Form 41 so your bank applies 10%, not 30%.
  3. File an Indian return if you have Indian income, to claim back excess TDS.
  4. If you were resident in India in any year while holding a Russian bank account or assets that you did not report, check FAST-DS before 31 December 2026: see FAST-DS for NRIs in Russia.

Frequently asked questions

What tax does an NRI in Russia pay in India?
Only tax on Indian income such as NRO interest, rent, dividends and gains on Indian assets. Income earned in Russia is not taxed in India while you are a non-resident.
Is my Russian salary taxable in India in the year I move?
It can be. If you were in India for 182 days or more in the financial year you moved, you are resident for that whole year, so your Russian salary until 31 March is also taxable in India, with credit for the Russian tax paid on it (claimed with Form 67), and your Russian bank accounts go in Schedule FA. From the first full year in which you are non-resident, it is not taxed in India.
What is the India-Russia DTAA rate on interest and dividends?
10% on interest, dividends and royalties, if you give your Indian payer a Russian tax residency certificate and Form 41. Without them, NRO interest suffers 30% plus surcharge and cess.
Is NRE interest taxable for an Indian living in Russia?
Not in India while you are a non-resident. It may be reportable in Russia if you are a Russian tax resident, because Russia taxes residents on worldwide income.
Can India tax my gains on Indian shares if I live in Russia?
Yes. The India-Russia treaty leaves India the right to tax gains on shares of Indian companies and on property in India.
Can I send money from India to Russia?
Yes, through banking channels, but transfers involving Russia face closer checks since 2022 and can be slower. NRO funds can be repatriated up to USD 1 million a year with a CA certificate and Form 145.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
