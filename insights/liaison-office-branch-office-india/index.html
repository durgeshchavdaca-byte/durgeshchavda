Foreign company guide

Liaison, branch or project office in India

Foreign companies can be present in India without forming an Indian company — but RBI decides what each type of office may do. Here are the current rules and what they cost you each year.

By CA Durgesh Chavda·Last reviewed 22 October 2026·6 min read
Plan your India office hello@durgeshchavda.com
Key takeaways
  • A liaison office needs 3 years of profits and USD 50,000 net worth; a branch 5 years and USD 100,000.
  • A liaison office cannot earn income; it is generally valid for 3 years.
  • From tax year 2026-27 the liaison office statement is Form 162 (formerly Form 49C).

What is a liaison office in India?

A liaison office is a representative office a foreign company opens in India with permission processed by its bank under RBI rules. It can only act as a communication channel — representing the parent, promoting its products and collaboration — and cannot earn income or do business. A branch office, by contrast, can carry on permitted commercial activities and is taxed as a foreign company.

A company that doesn’t meet the track record can apply with a letter of comfort from its parent.

What a liaison office can — and can’t — do

  • Can: represent the parent, promote exports and imports, promote technical or financial collaboration, and act as a communication channel.
  • Can’t: trade, sign contracts as a business, earn any income or borrow. Its costs are met from the head office’s remittances.
  • Validity is generally three years (two for NBFCs and construction companies); the bank can extend it if annual activity certificates are up to date.

When you need prior RBI approval

  • Applicants from Pakistan — always.
  • Applicants from Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan, Iran, China, Hong Kong or Macau — for offices in Jammu & Kashmir, the North-East or Andaman & Nicobar.
  • Defence, telecom, private security and information & broadcasting businesses; NGOs and foreign government bodies.
  • Sectors not open to 100% FDI under the automatic route go through the government.
  • Everything else is processed by your authorised dealer bank.

Annual compliance

  • Annual activity certificate for the year to 31 March, certified by a CA, to the bank and the income-tax department (International Taxation).
  • Liaison offices: the annual statement to the income-tax department — Form 162 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (formerly Form 49C), due within eight months of the year end.
  • Branch and project offices: audited accounts, income-tax return, transfer pricing and GST as applicable.

A change on the way

RBI published draft regulations in October 2025 that would drop the profit and net-worth tests in favour of a list of prohibited activities. Until they are notified, the rules above apply.

Office or subsidiary?

Most foreign companies that plan to sell in India choose a subsidiary — it’s taxed at a lower rate and limits liability. Compare options in the structure chooser.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a liaison office and a branch office in India?
A liaison office is only a communication channel and cannot earn income; a branch office can carry on permitted commercial activities and is taxed as a foreign company. A branch needs five years of profits and USD 100,000 net worth; a liaison office three years and USD 50,000.
How long is a liaison office valid in India?
Generally three years (two years for NBFCs and construction/development companies). The authorised dealer bank can extend it, provided annual activity certificates have been filed.
What replaced Form 49C?
From tax year 2026-27, a liaison office files its annual statement in Form 162 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026, within eight months of the end of the year.
Does a liaison office pay tax in India?
It should have no income, so normally no tax — but it must file annual statements with the income-tax department and an annual activity certificate with its bank.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
