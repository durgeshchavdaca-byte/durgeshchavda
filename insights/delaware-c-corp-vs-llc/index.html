Delaware C-Corp vs LLC: which should an Indian founder choose?

The right answer depends on one question more than any other: will you raise venture capital?

By CA Durgesh Chavda·Last reviewed 26 September 2026·8 min read
Get entity advice hello@durgeshchavda.com

The short answer

  • Raising VC, using SAFEs or granting stock options? Choose a Delaware C-Corp.
  • Small, owner-run business with no outside investors? An LLC can work — but as an India-resident owner, understand the US filings and Indian tax treatment first.

Side-by-side

When a C-Corp is right

If there is any realistic chance you will raise from US investors, choose the C-Corp from day one. Converting an LLC later costs legal fees and can trigger tax, and investors will ask for it anyway. A C-Corp also gives you clean founder vesting and an option pool.

When an LLC can work

A single-member LLC can suit a consulting or services business, a US storefront for an Indian product, or a holding vehicle — where the owners don’t need outside equity. Remember the Form 5472 obligation, which applies even with no revenue.

How India sees each

This is where LLCs get tricky. The US may ignore the LLC for tax, but Indian tax law generally looks at it as a separate foreign entity. That mismatch can make it harder to claim foreign tax credit in India for US tax paid, and changes how income is reported. In both cases, Indian residents must disclose the holding in Schedule FA of their income-tax return, and the investment must follow FEMA’s overseas investment rules. More: FEMA, ODI & tax rules for US companies.

Converting later

An LLC can convert to a C-Corp, and Delaware makes the legal step straightforward. The tax and FEMA consequences are the part to plan — ideally before the round that requires it.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner own a Delaware C-Corp?
Yes. There is no citizenship or residency requirement for shareholders, directors or officers of a Delaware corporation.
What is the dividend withholding rate for Indian shareholders of a US C-Corp?
Under the India–US tax treaty, US withholding on dividends is generally 15% if the beneficial owner is a company holding at least 10% of voting stock, and 25% in other cases, subject to treaty conditions.
Do VCs invest in LLCs?
Rarely. Most venture funds and accelerators require a Delaware C-Corp.
Is an LLC cheaper to run from India?
Delaware's annual LLC tax is a flat $400 (from 2026), but a foreign-owned LLC must still file a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 each year, and the Indian tax treatment can be more complex.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
