Converting your LLC to a Delaware C-Corp before you raise

You started with an LLC because it was cheap and simple. Now an investor wants a Delaware C-Corp. The conversion is routine in Delaware — the parts that need care are tax, timing and the Indian side.

By CA Durgesh Chavda·Last reviewed 26 September 2026·7 min read
Plan your conversion hello@durgeshchavda.com

Why convert

  • Investors: most venture funds and accelerators invest only in Delaware C-Corps, often on SAFEs.
  • Equity for the team: stock options are built around corporations.
  • Simpler filings for foreign owners: a C-Corp files its own Form 1120 instead of the LLC’s pro forma return — though Form 5472 still applies if 25%+ foreign-owned.

Background: Delaware C-Corp vs LLC.

Ways to convert

RouteHow it worksTypical use
Statutory conversionThe LLC becomes a corporation by filing a certificate of conversion; same legal entity, contracts and bank account continueDelaware LLC → Delaware C-Corp (the usual route)
Conversion across statesA Wyoming (or other state) LLC converts or domesticates into a Delaware corporation where both states permit itNon-Delaware LLCs
New corporation + contributionForm a new C-Corp and have the LLC or its members transfer assets or interests into itComplex histories or where conversion isn’t available
Tax election onlyElect for the LLC to be taxed as a corporation (Form 8832) without changing its legal formRarely enough for investors, who want an actual corporation

Delaware statutory conversion — the steps

  1. Approve the conversion as the LLC agreement requires (usually the members), together with a plan of conversion.
  2. Prepare the corporate charter — authorised shares and par value chosen with the franchise tax in mind.
  3. File the certificate of conversion and the certificate of incorporation together with the Division of Corporations (check current fees — Delaware revised many from 1 August 2026).
  4. Adopt bylaws, appoint directors and officers, and issue shares to the former members in proportion to their interests.
  5. Founder vesting and 83(b): if shares are subject to new vesting, consider 83(b) elections within 30 days.

US tax treatment

  • Converting an LLC into a corporation is commonly treated as a tax-free incorporation under Section 351, where the former members receive stock and together own at least 80% of the corporation immediately afterwards.
  • A single-member LLC that was a disregarded entity becomes a separate corporate taxpayer from the conversion date — two short tax periods may need to be reported for that year.
  • The corporation generally keeps the same EIN after a statutory conversion, but its tax classification changes — tell the IRS, your bank and payment platforms, and give clients a new form if they held a W-8 or W-9 for the LLC.
  • Foreign-owned: the final pro forma 1120 + Form 5472 for the LLC period, then Form 1120 + 5472 for the corporation. See Form 5472.

Timing and QSBS

US tax rules on qualified small business stock (QSBS) start the holding period from when the corporation issues its stock — for a converted LLC, from the conversion — so earlier conversion is usually better for US-taxpayer shareholders. For founders who remain Indian tax residents, QSBS matters less, but investors will still prefer you convert before, not during, the round.

The India side

  • If you’re India-resident, your LLC interest was an overseas investment; after conversion you hold shares instead. Inform your AD bank of the change so the ODI record (UIN) reflects the new instrument, and keep the conversion documents on file.
  • Your Annual Performance Report continues each year for the same foreign entity.
  • Update Schedule FA in your income-tax return to show shares rather than an LLC interest, and review how the conversion is treated for Indian tax.

More: FEMA, ODI & tax rules for US companies.

After the conversion — checklist

  • Update the bank, payment platforms, registered agent and any state registrations.
  • Issue share certificates or book entries and set up a cap table.
  • Adopt an option plan before hiring on equity.
  • Diarise the new Delaware deadline — the corporate annual report and franchise tax on 1 March replaces the LLC’s June tax. See the compliance calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Delaware LLC convert into a C-Corp?
Yes. Delaware permits a statutory conversion: the LLC files a certificate of conversion together with a certificate of incorporation and continues as the same legal entity, keeping its contracts and assets.
Is converting an LLC to a C-Corp taxable in the US?
It is commonly treated as a tax-free incorporation under Section 351 when the former members receive stock and together own at least 80% of the corporation immediately after the conversion — but review your facts, especially liabilities in excess of basis.
Does the company keep its EIN after converting?
In a statutory conversion the corporation generally keeps the same EIN, but its tax classification changes, so the IRS, banks and counterparties should be updated.
What do India-resident owners need to do?
Their LLC interest becomes shares in the corporation. They should inform their AD bank so the overseas investment record reflects the new instrument, continue filing the APR, and update Schedule FA in their Indian tax return.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
