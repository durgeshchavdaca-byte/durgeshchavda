Living in the UK? Your flat in India may now be in UK inheritance tax

Until April 2025, many Indians in the UK kept their Indian assets outside UK inheritance tax because their domicile was India. That shield is gone. The test is now how long you have lived in the UK, and once you pass it, property and money in India count towards the 40% tax.

Key takeaways
  • Since 6 April 2025, UK resident 10 of the last 20 years = long-term resident: Indian assets are in UK inheritance tax.
  • India has no inheritance tax; the heir pays capital gains only on a later sale.
  • The UK status continues 3 to 10 years after you leave.
Quick answer: from 6 April 2025, if you have been UK tax resident for at least 10 of the previous 20 tax years, you are a long-term UK resident and UK inheritance tax applies to your worldwide assets, including a flat, land, fixed deposits, mutual funds and shares in India. India has had no estate duty since 1985, so there is no Indian tax to offset it. The status follows you for 3 to 10 years after you leave the UK.

Who is caught: the 10-of-20-years test

Your situationIndian assets in UK inheritance tax?
UK resident fewer than 10 of the last 20 tax yearsNo; only UK assets are in scope
UK resident 10 or more of the last 20 tax yearsYes, worldwide assets, including India
Left the UK after being a long-term residentYes for a further 3 to 10 tax years, depending on how long you lived there
Non-resident for 10 consecutive tax yearsNo; the test resets

Citizenship and domicile no longer decide this. An Indian passport holder who moved to London in 2014 is likely caught; so is a British citizen of Indian origin with a family home in Pune.

What the UK counts, and what it costs

  • Everything in India you own outright: flats and land (your share if jointly owned), NRO and NRE deposits, mutual funds, demat shares, PPF balances and money owed to you.
  • The allowances: the first £325,000 of the whole estate is at 0%, plus up to £175,000 more if your main home goes to children or grandchildren. Assets left to a spouse or civil partner are generally exempt.
  • Above that: 40%, on the market value at death converted to pounds. A flat in Mumbai worth ₹2 crore adds around adds roughly £180,000 to the estatepound;170,000 to adds roughly £180,000 to the estatepound;180,000 to the estate at recent exchange rates.
  • Gifts: giving the flat to a child in India is tax-free in India, but for UK inheritance tax it only falls out of your estate if you live seven more years.

What happens on the Indian side

StepIndian tax position
Inheriting the assetNo inheritance or estate tax; the heir pays no income tax on receiving it
Transfer into the heir’s nameWill or succession certificate, then mutation in land records; stamp duty rules vary by state
Selling it laterCapital gains for the heir, using the original owner’s cost and holding period. A non-resident seller faces TDS on the gross amount unless a lower-deduction certificate is obtained
Sending the money to the UKFrom an NRO account, up to USD 1 million per financial year with Forms 145 and 146 (the old 15CA and 15CB)

Five things to do now

  1. Count your UK tax years. If you are at 8 or 9 of the last 20, you are close to the line.
  2. List every Indian asset with its rough value: property, deposits, funds, shares. Executors in the UK will need values in pounds at the date of death.
  3. Make sure there is a will that works in India for Indian property, so heirs avoid a long succession process.
  4. Decide whether to keep, gift or sell Indian property while alive. Selling in India has its own tax and TDS; gifting starts the UK seven-year clock.
  5. Talk to a UK adviser on the UK side. We handle the Indian side: valuations, title papers, sale, TDS, and getting the money out. See selling property in India from the UK.

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay UK inheritance tax on property in India?
Yes, if you are a long-term UK resident: UK tax resident for at least 10 of the previous 20 tax years. Since 6 April 2025 that test, not domicile, brings your worldwide assets, including Indian property, into UK inheritance tax.
Does India have inheritance tax?
No. India abolished estate duty in 1985, and an heir pays no income tax on receiving an inheritance. Tax arises only when the heir later sells the asset or earns income from it.
I leave the UK and move back to India. When do my Indian assets fall out of UK inheritance tax?
Not immediately. A long-term UK resident stays in scope for 3 to 10 tax years after leaving, depending on how many years they were UK resident. After 10 consecutive non-resident years the test resets.
Can I gift my flat in India to my children to avoid UK inheritance tax?
A gift to a relative is tax-free in India, but for UK inheritance tax it leaves your estate only if you survive seven years after the gift. Get UK advice before you transfer.
How does an heir abroad get the sale money out of India?
From an NRO account, up to USD 1 million per financial year, with Forms 145 and 146 (the old Forms 15CA and 15CB) and proof of inheritance.
