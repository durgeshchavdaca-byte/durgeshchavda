TDS rate chart 2026-27: every common rate and threshold

The new Act rewrote TDS into tables but kept the rates. Here’s the full chart for payments to residents and the key rates for non-residents.

By CA Durgesh Chavda·Last reviewed 7 October 2026·7 min read
Get TDS handled hello@durgeshchavda.com

How TDS works under the new Act

Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, the 194-series sections were replaced by tables: section 393(1) for payments to residents, 393(2) for non-residents and 393(3) for certain payments to anyone. Each row carries a payment code quoted in TDS returns. Rates and thresholds were carried over from the Budget 2025 changes.

No PAN? TDS is generally 20% (5% for 194Q / 194O items).

Payments to non-residents — section 393(2)

  • Interest, royalty and fees for technical services: 20%; long-term capital gains: 12.5%; short-term gains on listed equity: 20%; other income: 30% for individuals — plus surcharge and cess, or the lower treaty rate with a TRC and Form 41.
  • Buying property from an NRI: from 1 October 2026, resident individual buyers deposit TDS against their PAN without a TAN — see TDS on NRI property sale.
  • Full guide: TDS on payments to non-residents.

What Budget 2026 changed

  • Manpower supply is treated as contract work (1% / 2%).
  • No-TAN TDS for individuals buying property from NRIs.
  • TCS on overseas tour packages: flat 2%; on education and medical remittances above ₹10 lakh: 2% — see the TCS calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the TDS rate on professional fees in 2026-27?
10% above ₹50,000 a year; technical fees and call-centre payments are 2% above ₹50,000.
What is the TDS on rent for 2026-27?
10% on rent for land, buildings or furniture and 2% on plant and machinery, where rent exceeds ₹50,000 a month; individuals and HUFs not under audit deduct 2% above ₹50,000 a month.
What is the TDS threshold for bank interest?
₹50,000 a year (₹1 lakh for senior citizens), with TDS at 10%.
What is the TDS rate on contractor payments?
1% for individual or HUF contractors and 2% for others, where a single payment exceeds ₹30,000 or the year's total exceeds ₹1 lakh.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
