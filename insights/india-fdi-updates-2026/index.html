Frequently asked questions

What changed for foreign investors in India in 2026?
Among others: the Income-tax Act 2025 took effect on 1 April 2026; Press Note 2 (2026) eased rules for investors with up to 10% non-controlling land-border ownership; from June 2026 any individual abroad can buy listed Indian shares directly; insurance moved to 100% automatic-route FDI; and trade agreements with the UK and New Zealand took effect.
Is the India–EU trade agreement in force?
Not yet. Negotiations concluded on 27 January 2026 and signature is targeted for December 2026.

Please note: this guide is general information based on the rules as they stood on the review date above. Regulations and RBI/CBDT procedures change often — please get advice on your specific facts before acting.
