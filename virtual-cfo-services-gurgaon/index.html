Virtual CFO services in Gurgaon & Delhi NCR — CFO-grade finance, part-time

Gurugram’s SaaS, D2C, fintech and mobility founders — and the Indian arms of foreign groups across Delhi NCR — use us as their finance team: monthly close, board reporting, fundraising and compliance.

What you get

  • Monthly close, MIS and board packs — numbers your investors and auditors can rely on.
  • Runway, budgets and unit economics — with a model you can actually use in fundraising.
  • Fundraising support — model, data room and diligence; Durgesh led a USD 3.75M Pre-Series A end-to-end. See VC funding advisory.
  • FEMA and RBI filings when foreign investors come in — FC-GPR, FC-TRS, FLA.
  • GST, TDS and ROC oversight, plus AI-assisted close and reconciliations.

Who it suits

  • Seed to Series B startups in SaaS, D2C, fintech and mobility across Gurugram, Delhi and Noida.
  • Indian subsidiaries and capability centres of US, European and Japanese groups — see Indian subsidiary compliance.
  • Founders with a US parent or planning a Delaware flip.

Working with Gurgaon teams

  • Multi-state GST: NCR companies often have registrations in Haryana, Delhi and Uttar Pradesh — we keep each state’s returns and input credit reconciled.
  • Foreign-parent reporting: monthly packs mapped to US GAAP or IFRS, transfer pricing and the repatriation questions that come with them.

Delivery is remote-first — shared books, weekly calls and a monthly review — with visits to Gurgaon for board meetings, audits or investor diligence when needed. Our offices are in Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Rajkot and Dubai.
