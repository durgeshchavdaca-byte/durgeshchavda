Forensic audit

Forensic audit & fraud investigation in India — find out what really happened

When numbers don’t add up — a whistleblower email, unexplained cash leakage, a supplier nobody has met, or doubts after an acquisition — a forensic audit establishes the facts from the books, the bank trail and the people. We work for boards, foreign parents of Indian subsidiaries, investors and lenders.

Confidential first call hello@durgeshchavda.com

When you need a forensic audit

  • A whistleblower complaint or an anonymous email alleging fraud or bribery.
  • Margins, cash or inventory that don’t reconcile — and nobody can explain why.
  • Suspected kickbacks, fake vendors or inflated invoices in purchasing.
  • Funds moved to related parties or diverted from their stated purpose.
  • Doubts about an Indian business after you invested in or acquired it.
  • A lender, investor or board asking for an independent forensic review.

How an engagement works

01

Scope in confidence

A call with you — and your lawyers, where needed — to agree the allegations, the questions and who must not be alerted.

02

Preserve and analyse

Secure books, bank statements, email and records; analyse transactions, match them to public records and test patterns.

03

Interview and report

Interviews where appropriate, then a written report of facts, evidence and amounts — clear enough for a board, a court or an insurer.

We follow the Forensic Accounting and Investigation Standards issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, and work with specialist partners for digital forensics where devices need to be imaged. Legal advice and any proceedings stay with your lawyers; we often work under their instruction.

Frequently asked questions

What is a forensic audit in India?
A forensic audit examines a company's books, bank trail, records and people to establish whether fraud, diversion of funds or other misconduct occurred, and to quantify it — producing evidence-based findings for a board, investor, lender, insurer or court.
How is a forensic audit different from a statutory audit?
A statutory audit gives an opinion on whether financial statements are true and fair. A forensic audit investigates specific allegations or risks in depth — tracing transactions, matching records to third-party data and interviewing people — to establish facts and amounts.
Can you investigate our Indian subsidiary for a foreign parent?
Yes. We investigate on the ground in India and report in English, typically working with the parent's counsel so the findings support decisions under the FCPA, the UK Bribery Act or similar laws.
Is a forensic investigation confidential?
Yes. Scope, access and communication are agreed with you — and your lawyers where needed — so that the people involved are not alerted before evidence is secured.
