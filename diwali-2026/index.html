Diwali gifts

The ₹50,000 rule

Tax-freeGifts from relatives, of any value: parents, siblings, spouse, children and other relatives as defined in the law.
TaxableGifts from friends or others, once the year’s total crosses ₹50,000. Then the whole amount is taxed, not just the excess.
